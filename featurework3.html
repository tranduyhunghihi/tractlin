In a world of constant change and self-discovery, we are all confronted with a fundamental question: is there a core essence of “me” that remains constant, or are we simply a collection of evolving experiences?
This design project delves into the inevitable identity crisis, exploring the tension between transformation and the persistent yearning for a stable sense of self.
By examining how we grapple with change, This project seeks to illuminate the ways in which we can embrace growth while honoring the fundamental aspects of our emotions and who we are.
